# Decisions

Dated records of choices, one per file, in the format **Context**, **Options considered**, **Decision**, **Consequences**, **Status** (template in [how to document](../guides/how-to-document.md#templates)). A record is never edited after acceptance except its status line and its **Supersedes** section; a change of mind is a new record that supersedes the old one (see [superseding a decision](../guides/how-to-track-changes.md#superseding-a-decision)). This index lists decisions in force; superseded ones are deleted or archived per the retention directive. Standing instructions are summarised in [user directives](../product/user-directives.md).
